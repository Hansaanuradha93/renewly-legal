Renewly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Data Not Collected

Renewly is a private subscription and bill tracker. It has no account, no bank connection and no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code. This page says exactly what that means.

What Renewly collects

Nothing. The developer does not collect, receive, store or share any personal data from the app. There is no sign-up, no login, no server that holds your information, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your iPhone

The subscriptions and bills you add (names, amounts, dates, notes, categories), your alert settings and whether Pro is unlocked are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read that same on-device storage; they do not send it anywhere.

Notifications

Renewly's reminders are local notifications scheduled and delivered by your iPhone. Renewly asks for permission to send them only when the feature needs it, and you can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings. Nothing about your reminders leaves your device.

Purchases

If you buy Renewly Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple provides the developer only aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics (for example, number of downloads and trial starts).

Diagnostics

Renewly includes no crash-reporting service. If you have chosen to share iPhone analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may make aggregated crash and performance reports available to the developer through Xcode Organizer. Renewly also logs diagnostics on your device only; they are never sent anywhere, and item names and amounts are marked private in those logs.

Service logos and links

Any service logos shown in Renewly are bundled inside the app; Renewly does not download them. The "how to cancel" help opens the service's own website in your browser only when you tap it, and that site's privacy policy then applies.

Children

Renewly is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Renewly ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the date above will change. A change that collects data would also change the app's App Store privacy label.
