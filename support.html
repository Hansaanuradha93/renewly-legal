Renewly Support

Renewly is a private subscription and bill tracker with no account and no server, so there's nothing to look up on our end — but we're happy to help.

Contact

Email hansaanuradha93@gmail.com with any question, bug report or feature request.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone only, inside the app's own storage. There's no account to lose and no sync to go wrong.

How do I restore Pro? Settings → Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not an account with us.

How do I cancel a subscription I'm tracking? Renewly only tracks what you tell it; cancelling the subscription itself happens with the service you subscribed to, not in Renewly.
